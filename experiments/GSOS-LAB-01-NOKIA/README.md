# GSOS-LAB-01 — Nokia Case Study

## Observation
Observation of a significant change/event involving Nokia.

## Evidence
Evidence is recorded with source, timestamp, provenance and verification status.

## Analysis
GSOS analyzes the observed evidence and determines its confidence and consistency.

## Decision
The Decision Engine produces a controlled decision:
NO_ACTION / START_OBSERVATION / STOP_OBSERVATION

## Audit
Every observation, evidence item, analysis, decision and result must be reproducible.
